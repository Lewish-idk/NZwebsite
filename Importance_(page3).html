Important New Zealand Exports

Red meat is also a major export of New Zealand and accounts for 1 in 20 jobs. The industry profits aproximately $17.5 billiion annually, with over 90% of products being sold overseas. Our fresh meat produce is a key part of the enconomy and a delicous diet option.
Dairy is New Zealand's single biggest export, valuing $24.3 billion and contributing to 30% of foreign income. This is due to our large production of dairy from countless farms across NZ. Milk, Butter, Cheese and yogurt are all major products that are shipped to many countries world wide.
